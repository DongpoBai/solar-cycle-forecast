Helioseismic Frequency Shifts as Leading Indicators of Solar Activity¶

Research Question¶

Can solar p-mode oscillation frequency shifts predict solar activity before it manifests on the surface?

Solar acoustic oscillations (p-modes) are sensitive to conditions in the solar interior. As the magnetic cycle evolves, the near-surface magnetic field alters the acoustic cavity, causing measurable shifts in oscillation frequencies. If these interior changes precede surface manifestations (sunspots, radio emission), then helioseismic data could serve as a leading indicator for solar cycle forecasting.

We use 14 years of zero crossing data from the USC Solar Physics Group's HMI processing pipeline (Runs 1–74, covering 2010–2024) to investigate this hypothesis.

1. Load and Inspect Zero Crossing Data¶

Each "run" in the pipeline processes 72 days of HMI observations. The zero crossing value represents the frequency (in µHz) at which the solar p-mode frequency shift changes sign relative to a reference model — essentially measuring how much the oscillation frequencies have shifted due to solar magnetic activity.

Higher zero crossing frequency → more magnetically active Sun.

4. Lead-Lag Analysis¶

This is the core scientific analysis. We compute the cross-correlation between frequency shifts and ISN at different time lags.

  • Lag = 0: frequency shift and ISN measured simultaneously
  • Lag = +1: frequency shift leads ISN by 1 run (72 days)
  • Lag = -1: ISN leads frequency shift by 1 run

If the peak correlation occurs at a positive lag, it means frequency shifts predict future ISN — the result we're looking for.

5. Frequency Shifts as Predictive Features¶

Regardless of whether there's a lead, we can test whether adding frequency shift data to our XGBoost model improves predictions. We compare:

  • Model A: XGBoost with standard activity indices only
  • Model B: XGBoost with standard indices + frequency shifts

Since zero crossings are available every 72 days, we forward-fill them to daily resolution for the ML model.

7. Summary and Scientific Implications¶

Findings:¶

  1. P-mode frequency shifts strongly correlate with solar activity (r ≈ 0.6–0.9), confirming the known physical relationship between interior magnetic fields and surface manifestations.

  2. Lead-lag analysis reveals whether frequency shifts lead ISN. (Results from Section 4 above — interpret based on actual numbers.)

  3. Adding helioseismic features to XGBoost (did / did not) improve prediction accuracy, suggesting that p-mode data (does / does not) carry information beyond traditional activity indices.

Physical interpretation:¶

The solar magnetic cycle originates in the interior (likely at the tachocline, ~0.7 R☉). P-mode frequencies are sensitive to near-surface magnetic fields. Sunspots are a surface phenomenon. If frequency shifts lead sunspot numbers, it would support the picture of magnetic flux rising from the interior to the surface with a measurable time delay.

Limitations:¶

  • Only ~60 good data points spanning 1.5 solar cycles
  • 72-day temporal resolution limits our ability to detect short leads
  • Zero crossing quality varies across runs (processing artifacts)
  • The forward-fill approach for daily ML features is crude

Future work:¶

  • Extend analysis as more runs are processed (SC25 is ongoing)
  • Use individual 3-day frequency shifts instead of 72-day averages for finer temporal resolution
  • Apply to MDI data for longer historical coverage
  • Investigate whether specific l-ranges show stronger leading behavior
